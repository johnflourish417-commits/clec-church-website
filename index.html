About Our Church

Christ Life Empowerment Center is committed to empowering people and raising a godly army. Under the leadership of Pastor Paul D. John, we seek to raise people of impact in every sphere of life and equip them with a spirit of excellence through teaching, preaching, and the demonstration of God's power.

Our Vision

Empowering people to attain the fullness of Christ life and raising a godly army through the discovery and actualisation of purpose and destiny for life there for building nations of the world.

Our Mission

To raise people of impact in all fears of life fill and endown with the spirt of excellence through teaching, preaching and the demonstration of God's power.

Our Community

A place for worship, prayer, fellowship, spiritual growth, and the love of God.
